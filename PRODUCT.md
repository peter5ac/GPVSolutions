# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing codebase: static HTML pages (index.html, support.html, privacy.html) with inline CSS and a small contact.js; hosted under a custom domain (CNAME).

## Users

Shop owners, solo operators and IT or operations leads at manufacturers who run CNC machines, evaluating the CNC Backup app and choosing between the Individual and Fleet plans. Support visitors are existing customers looking for setup help or an answer. (Audience inferred from the page copy and the app's PRODUCT.md; not separately interviewed.)

## Product Purpose

This site sells and supports CNC Backup, a subscription iPad and iPhone app that backs up CNC controller files to SharePoint, Google Drive or Dropbox, even when the shop floor is offline. It must let a visitor understand what the app does, compare plans, pick one, and find support and the privacy policy. Success is a visitor choosing a plan with an accurate understanding of what it includes.

## Positioning

One-button, offline-tolerant CNC backup with machine detection and dynamic folder naming. Fleet adds Microsoft SSO, a managed IT experience and an audit log. GPV Solutions LLC presents itself as "Industrial Software Systems".

## Operating Context

- Pages: home (pitch, plan comparison table, plan selection, support contact), support (setup help and FAQ), privacy policy.
- Visitors may arrive from a phone or tablet in a shop, or from an office desktop.
- Support hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, via the "Ask a question" button on the page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plans compared: Individual (one device, license key) and Fleet (Microsoft SSO, optional managed IT experience, audit log with managed policy).
- Claims must match the app: upload verification checks the cloud copy against the checksum and size the provider reports; Dropbox is checked by file size only.
- Clarify and polish work must preserve factual copy; ask before changing claims or plan contents.
- Pricing and checkout mechanics are not recorded here; confirm in the code before editing.

## Brand Commitments

Names: "CNC Backup" (product) and "GPV Solutions" (company). Current look is dark navy header (#0F172A), blue accent (#2563EB) and a system font stack; treat it as the incumbent, not a binding commitment, until a design direction is established.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer testimonials, case studies, screenshots or logos are in the repo. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Accurate over impressive: every claim states exactly what the app does.
2. Make the Individual vs Fleet decision easy to reach and hard to misread.
3. Readable on a phone in a shop and on an office desktop.
4. Support and privacy are easy to find; trust is part of the sale.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific standard established; aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-operable controls as a baseline (suggested, unconfirmed).
