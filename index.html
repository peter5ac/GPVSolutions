CNC Backup: Machine Recovery Solution for Modern Manufacturing

Choose Your Plan

CNC Backup is a subscription app for iPad and iPhone that backs up CNC controller files from USB drives to Dropbox, Google Drive or SharePoint, even when the shop floor is offline.

Plans, SSO & Managed Fleet

Individual
One user, one subscription. Activate with the license key emailed after purchase.
Fleet
For shops with many operators and devices, with unlimited seats. Activate with a license key, or with SSO.
Single Sign-On
Fleet supports Microsoft Entra ID SSO. Your team signs in with work accounts, so there are no keys to distribute, and SharePoint uploads are authorized at the same time.
Managed Fleet
Admins set one SharePoint destination, folder naming and required labels for the whole organization, and review an audit log of backups. Operators just plug in and tap Upload.

Licenses are cached securely, so backups keep working offline. Interested in SSO or a managed setup? Email support@gpvsolutions.com.
